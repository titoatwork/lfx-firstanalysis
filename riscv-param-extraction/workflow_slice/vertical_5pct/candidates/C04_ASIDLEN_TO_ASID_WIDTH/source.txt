Subsection: Supervisor Address Translation and Protection (satp) Register
Anchor: norm:asidlen

The number of ASID bits is UNSPECIFIED and may be zero. The number of
implemented ASID bits, termed ASIDLEN, is also implementation-defined.
The maximal value of ASIDLEN, termed ASIDMAX, is 9 for Sv32 or 16 for
Sv39, Sv48, and Sv57.
